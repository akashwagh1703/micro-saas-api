# Career AI – End-to-End Candidate Journey

## Overview

Career AI is an intelligent career assistant that helps candidates discover relevant job opportunities, improve their resumes, prepare for interviews, and plan their career growth. The platform uses AI to analyze a candidate's resume, understand their preferences, match them with suitable jobs, and provide personalized career support throughout the job search journey.

---

## Step 1: Upload Resume

The candidate begins by uploading their latest resume in PDF or DOCX format.

### Purpose

This serves as the foundation for creating the candidate's professional profile and enables AI-driven analysis and recommendations.

### Supported Formats

* PDF
* DOCX

### Outcome

The resume is securely processed and prepared for AI analysis.

---

## Step 2: AI Resume Parsing & Skill Extraction

Career AI automatically analyzes the uploaded resume and extracts key professional information.

### Information Extracted

* Skills
* Work Experience
* Education
* Current Role
* Industry
* Certifications
* Technical Competencies
* Professional Summary

### Example

Skills:

* Laravel
* PHP
* React
* MySQL

Experience:

* 4 Years

Current Role:

* PHP Developer

Industry:

* Information Technology

### Outcome

The system generates a structured professional profile from the resume data.

## Step 3: Collect Candidate Preferences

To provide highly relevant job recommendations, Career AI collects additional career preferences from the candidate.

### Required Information

#### Location Preferences

* Current Location
* Preferred Location(s)

#### Compensation Preferences

* Current Salary (CTC)
* Expected Salary

#### Availability

* Notice Period

#### Work Preferences

* Employment Type (Full-Time, Contract, Part-Time)
* Work Mode (Remote, Hybrid, Onsite)

Preferred Locations:

* Mumbai
* Remote

Current Salary:
₹6 LPA

Expected Salary:
₹10 LPA

30 Days

Hybrid

### Outcome

The platform gains additional context required for accurate job matching.

## Step 6: Resume Analysis & Match Scoring

Career AI evaluates how well the candidate matches each job opportunity.

### Match Score Components

* Skills Match
* Experience Match
* Location Match
* Salary Match
* Notice Period Match

### Formula

Total Match Score =
Skills +
Experience +
Location +
Salary +
Notice Period

### Score Categories

95% – 100%
Excellent Match

80% – 94%
Good Match

60% – 79%
Partial Match

Below 60%
Low Match

### Outcome

Candidates understand their strengths, weaknesses, and likelihood of success for each job opportunity.
